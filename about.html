About us

IFFCO Kisan Logistics Limited (IKLL) is a special purpose vehicle (SPV) established in Kandla, Kutch, (Gujarat) in order to handle the BOT (Build, Operate, Transfer) project as per the captive policy of Government of India and as directed by Kandla Port Trust.

IKLL provides all weather suitable barge jetty with berthing facilities for handling barges carrying cargo of 2000-2500 MT off Kandla creek, Gujarat. It provides suitable access point from barge jetty to existing factory boundary of IFFCO to transport raw materials by truck movement.

The materials handled are solid and non- hazardous including Muriate of Potash (MOP), Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP), Mono-Ammonium Phosphate (MAP) and Urea.

Kandla Port Trust provides right to use to IKLL for using barge jetty and allied facilities to berth barges cargo from mid stream unloading of large shipments.

IKLL have first right for berthing their barges on this jetty. IKLL shall provide minimum guaranteed cargo of 1 MMTPA of captive cargo consisting of potash, urea, DAP or MAP etc.

Mr. K. J. Patel

Chairman & Nominee Director

Mr. K. J. Patel is the Managing Director of IFFCO, he is a highly accomplished mechanical engineer with over four decades of specialized experience in the maintenance and operation of Nitrogenous and Phosphatic fertiliser plants. He holds an engineering degree from Saurashtra University, Gujarat.

Mr. Patel began his career at IFFCO's Kalol Unit in 1985 and later served as Director (Technical) at IFFCO, where he oversaw technical operations across all units. He is known for his strong domain expertise, strategic leadership, and hands-on approach to operational excellence.

Mr. Patel has represented IFFCO at several national and international forums, where he has presented papers and shared insights on advanced maintenance technologies. His contributions have been instrumental in shaping IFFCO's technical roadmap and enhancing its performance standards.
