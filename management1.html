Mr. Dileep Sanghani

Chairman

Mr. Dileep Sanghani is the Nominee Non- Executive Chairman of M/s IFFCO Kisan Logistics Limited. He is a well- known figure in the Cooperative sector with exceptional experience across agriculture, rural banking, insurance, cooperative governance, public administration and global cooperative diplomacy.

He has served four terms in Lok Sabha along with the several cabinet portfolios in the Government of Gujarat. He is serving as the Chairman of M/s Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Ltd. (IFFCO) and as Director on the Board of Indian Potash Ltd. (IPL), Jordan Indian Fertiliser Company LLC and Jordan Phosphate & Mines Company Ltd. He is also serving as Chairman of Gujarat State Cooperative Marketing Federation Ltd. (GUJCOMASOL), Amreli Jilla Madhyashtha Sahakari Bank Ltd, and National Institute of Rural Banking, Bengaluru.

He has also been awarded with several awards and recognitions including Life Time Achievement award at the Ingenious Banking Leadership Summit & Iconic Leaders Award (Goa, December, 2024) and has also been awarded with "Sahakarita Shiromani" Award by KRIBHCO.

Mr. K. J. Patel

Nominee Director

Mr. K. J. Patel is the Managing Director of IFFCO, he is a highly accomplished mechanical engineer with over four decades of specialized experience in the maintenance and operation of Nitrogenous and Phosphatic fertiliser plants. He holds an engineering degree from Saurashtra University, Gujarat.

Mr. Patel began his career at IFFCO's Kalol Unit in 1985 and later served as Director (Technical) at IFFCO, where he oversaw technical operations across all units. He is known for his strong domain expertise, strategic leadership, and hands-on approach to operational excellence.

Mr. Patel has represented IFFCO at several national and international forums, where he has presented papers and shared insights on advanced maintenance technologies. His contributions have been instrumental in shaping IFFCO's technical roadmap and enhancing its performance standards.

Mr. Ramesh Kumar

Nominee Director

Mr. Ramesh Kumar holds the position of Joint Managing Director & Chief Financial Officer in IFFCO. Mr Kapur is also responsible for the Finance Division of IFFCO. He is a Director on the Board of IFFCO's subsidiaries. He is also vested with the responsibility of Managing Director of IFFCO's subsidiaries like IFFCO Kisan Special Economic Zone (IKSEZ), Nellore and IFFCO Kisan Sanchar Limited (IKSL).

Mr. Kapur joined IFFCO on 5th October 2000 as Executive Director (Finance) and took over the charge of Finance Director with effect from November 7, 2001. He has been responsible for planning, managing and monitoring all finance and accounts related functions of IFFCO. He has also been extensively involved with all the new Projects, Acquisitions, Joint Ventures and Diversification initiatives of IFFCO from their stage of inception.

Mr. Ramesh Kumar is an ex-IRS Officer of 1978 Batch, who was holding the rank of Commissioner of Income Tax in his parent cadre before he was absorbed in IFFCO in October 2005. He was awarded the Best Probationer's Silver Medal in the IRS Batch by the National Academy of Direct Taxes, Nagpur. He has wide experience in Finance, Management, Business Strategising, Accounts and Taxation. He held various senior assignments in the Government of India, which included Joint Secretary (Commercial), Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI); Director in the Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Department of Fertilizers; Additional Assessor & Collector, Municipal Corporation of Delhi apart from the postings in the Income Tax Department as Additional Commissioner of Income Tax, Deputy / Jt. Commissioner of Income Tax since 1978. He had also worked in Technical Services Department of the Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (Marketing Division) during 1975-78.

Mr. Kapur was born on September 26, 1954. He holds B.Tech. Degree in Mechanical Engineering (in First Class with Distinction) from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), New Delhi and a Post Graduation in Management. He has travelled widely and participated in various national and international Seminars and Conferences, and presented papers on different subjects.

Mr. Yogendra Kumar

Nominee Director

He is in the process to develop nano fertilizer an improve fertilizer use efficiency.

Born on 5.08.1961, he has been a recipient of several awards and has also represented IFFCO in various national and international forums.
